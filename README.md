**Description:**

The project focuses on addressing urban mobility challenges in Mexico by developing a multi-agent traffic simulation system. The main objective relies in the idea to reduce traffic congestion through different strategies that will help to optimize the traffic through the traffic lights and parking spaces. The system is meant to simulate the dynamics between the cars and different elements in a 3D system that will be designed by the team. Proving the stategy to reduce pollution and promote better mobility for the citizens.
